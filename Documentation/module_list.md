# Talentra — Agreed Module List

## Phase 1: Common Modules

These modules form the shared foundation of the database.

1. **Master Data** — cities, states, countries, skills, qualifications, industries, job roles, employment types, work types, languages, reference codes, and UI translation keys.
2. **Profile** — candidate identity and contact details, education, experience, skills, languages, resumes, projects, assessments, course enrolments, profile history, and referrals.
3. **Jobs and Applications** — job requirements, positions, questionnaires, applications, status pipeline, shortlists, interviews, offers, joining records, and job-search history.

## Phase 2: Individual Modules

4. **Administration** — users, credentials, roles, permissions, identity sessions, audit trail, consent records, and blocking lists.
5. **Organisation and Account** — employers, client organisations, company details, locations, verification, recruiter mapping, account administrators, and organisation hierarchy.
6. **Gig Marketplace** — gig requests, gig worker profiles, availability, gig applications, assignments, handoff references, earnings references, lists, and ratings.

## Phase 3: Individual Modules

7. **Licences, Seats, Devices and Sessions** — licences, user and device limits, seat allocation, device registration, sessions, login history, and revocation.
8. **Sourcing and Consultant Requests** — sourcing requests, consultant eligibility, acceptance or decline, outreach batches, recipients, and candidate shortlists.
9. **Subscriptions, Wallet, Payments and Download History** — plans, subscriptions, renewals, payment orders, invoices, refunds, wallet ledger, credit consumption, and profile downloads.
10. **Notifications, Preferences and Community** — notification preferences and delivery, job alerts, language and privacy preferences, community posts, reactions, support tickets, FAQs, and referrals.
11. **Analytics Layer and Reporting Marts** — analytical dimensions, facts, and aggregates for the required KPIs.

## Optional Stretch Coverage

Salary benchmarks, company ratings, trending job snapshots, dashboard aggregates, and feature flags may be added separately if time permits.
